## New assets

## New assets

~ Added: variant of the vanilla western armor without mittens.

# Orthodox
~ Added: Orthodox monk robe.
~ Added: Orthodox omophorion.
~ Added: orthodox koukoulion.

# Muslim
~ Added: Caliphal Qalansuwah.
~ Added: black version of the vanilla Imam's robe.
~ Added: high nobility tagelmust.

#Jewish
~ Added: Jewish Judenhut.
~ Added: Jewish Sudra.

## AI Usage
~ Changed: Orthodox clergy wore no headgear before 1500's but monks.
~ Changed: bedouin and berbers dukes will use the new high nobility tagelmust.
~ Changed: catholic clergy is now shaved.
~ Changed: counts and below who are pilgrims, or other characters under temporary post-pilgrimage modifier effect, will sometimes display a pilgrim hat.
~ Changed: iranian and turkics muslim clergy will use a black robe and qalasuwah.
~ Changed: jewish courtiers will sometimes display a cultural headgear based on their own origin (sudra or judenhut).
~ Changed: northern and anglo-saxons bishops before high medieval era will use CFP early bishop outfit.
~ Changed: orthodox monks will use their own specific outfit with a new robe and koukoulion.
~ Changed: orthodox patriarch and bishops will now wear an omophorion.
~ Changed: orthodox patriarch to use orthodox bishop robe as vanilla robe is a moddern one.
~ Changed: pectoral cross to be assigned to northern catholic dukes and above with zealous/theologiah/devoted/faith_warrior trait.
~ Changed: pope's theocratic vassals, courtiers and court bishop will wear the cardinal outfit after 1245 (1st Council of Lyon).
~ Changed: vanilla pope outfit will only spawn once pope reached late medieval era. CFP early medieval outfit will be used prior to that.

## Expanded barbershop
~ Changed: african masks are now displayed with other religious headgears and with white icon.

## Backstage changes
~ Improved: material and colors of vanilla catholic monk robes.
~ Fixed: clipping on the high nobility tagelmust.
~ Fixed: most frequent occurences of ears and noses clipping throught helmets.
~ Fixed: sometimes, Orthodox clergy had no beard.
~ Fixed: western europe Normans used Siculo-norman helmets.
~ Fixed: white line on the noble pillbox hat.
