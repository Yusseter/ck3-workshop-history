Version 3.2.0

Tours and Tournaments Compatibility!
Community Flavor Pack is now compatible with achievements!
This changelog only lists visible changes and/or those meaningful to players and modders.

## Accessories
~ Fixed: various UV issues within some models.
~ Removed: CFP Norman Armor (ToTo model is better).
~ Removed: CFP Tabard plate armor until further investigation.
~ Removed: CFP deprecated Byzantine royal cloak.
~ Removed: CFP vanilla edited armors which are now included in ToTo (leather arabic armor, leather abbasid armor, byzantine golden armor).
~ Removed: mitenless armors until further notice.

## Royal Courts
~ Updated: Norman Royal Court stone material.

## Events
~ Updated: "event_background" value to "reference" in artifacts events.
~ Updated: scope = none to "cfp_historical_artifacts.0001".

## AI Usage
~ Changed: armors eras vanilla values set to more historical ones, they will spawn in 1150 and 1280 (no COA tabards in 1066!).
~ Changed: replaced EP1 Otto III crown by CFP model, with vanilla behavior.
~ Changed: replaced CFP timelock triggers by their vanilla counterparts. Does not require EP2.
~ Changed: CFP northern circlet in the new "northern_high_nobility" gene which only had a placeholder western circlet.
~ Changed: no more pillboxes in "northern_common".
~ Improved: if you have Fate of Iberia, CFP Iberian helmets are now assigned to commoners only.
~ Improved: Siculo-Norman helmet to fade out before era4 (1300).
~ Improved: Indian CFP helmets (tibetan and timurid) assigned to low nobles (moved to separate gene).
~ Improved: Indian kulavi hats assigned to era4 (1300+).
~ Improved: Latin Henin assigned to era 2 (1000-1125).
~ Improved: MENA mail coifs and stripped helmet are now assigned to commoners (moved to separate gene).
~ Improved: kettle hats helmets pushed to era4 armored commoners (1300+).
~ Improved: Aragonese dragon crest crown and helm pushed to era4 (1300+).
~ Improved: pillbox hats will appear less frequently in western europe.
~ Improved: byzantine roman helmets to appear only during era1 (before 1100).
~ Improved: title-locked crowns handling (vanilla safety triggers : not worn by babies, not worn when helmet should be on, etc.).
~ Improved: CFP Norse helmets portrait_modifiers to swap between common and fancy helmets depending on character's rank.
~ Improved: shield cloaks will trigger exactly like their corresponding helmets.
~ Improved: greatly simplified Turkics handling on code's side.
~ Improved: removed old CFP Normans handlers but still forcing Normands to dress up accurately all the time. Using EP2 assets, requires EP2.
~ Fixed: Normands won't carry a coppergate helmet anymore.
~ Fixed: clipping hairstyles in pilgrim hat.
~ Fixed: typo in code for spymaster prop animation.
~ Fixed: Jewish commoners clothing triggers to prevent jewish hat to spawn if character wears an armor.


## Expanded barbershop
~ Improved: Vanilla barbershop compatibility for animations and backgrounds.

## Game rules
~ Updated: game rules with "categories" value.
~ Removed: never_cfp_barbershop_unlocks game rule.

## Backstage changes
~ Changed: moved headgears modifiers into several files as per vanilla setup.
~ Changed: all clothing triggers classnames changed from "cfp_portrait_XXX" to "portrait_cfp_XXX" in order to match new vanilla systems.
~ Changed: "cfp_western_war_high_fullplate" to "cfp_western_era3_war_nobility".
~ Changed: "cfp_western_war_late" to "cfp_western_war_plate_armor".
~ Changed: "has_government" to "government_has_flag".
~ Changed: "hidden_effect_new_artifact" to "hidden_effect".
~ Changed: scope of all "portrait_cfp_XXX_clothing_trigger".
~ Updated: theocracy_government RoCo redefine with new "flag" value.
~ Improved: "cfp" prefix to CFP genes and portraits_modifiers to help with maintenance of compatibility patches.
~ Improved: GIANT code clean-up.
~ Improved: merged "cfp_western_crusades_black" into "cfp_western_crusades" and cleaned-up crusaders code.
~ Improved: merged all Jewish commoner hats in a single gene.
~ Improved: merged all plate armor helmets in a single gene.
~ Removed: CFP in-built time lock triggers.
~ Removed: deprecated classes.
~ Removed: deprecated clothing triggers.
~ Removed: deprecated models.
~ Removed: deprecated morphs in clothes.
~ Removed: deprecated turkic clothing genes.
~ Removed: portrait_cfp_wear_helmet_high_noble_trigger.

## Strings and translations
~ Removed: missing strings that are not missing anymore (know issue: EP2 vanilla legwears have no loc).
