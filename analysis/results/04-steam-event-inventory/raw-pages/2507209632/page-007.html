Update: 4 Jun, 2022 @ 7:39pm

##############################
Patch Notes: Castle Update/ Fate of Iberia / By the Emperor's Hand
##############################

COMPATIBILITY:

-Mod updated for 1.6.0 Castle Update/ Fate of Iberia

ADDITIONS:

-Wide variety of new Byzantine assets including imperial, royal, and noble headwear as well as some retextures of armor and vanilla assets including:
-Byzantine Royal Diadems
-Imperial Byzantine Female crowns
-New Male Byzantine Imperial Stemma
-New Nobility caps, hats, and retextures
-Roman Laurel Wreaths
-And more smaller edits!
-Historical Crown of Justinian model updated for Barbershop and Artifact.
-Crown of Theodora added to Barbershop.
-Crown of Zvonimir given to Croatian title holder.
-Some new variants of Abbasid turbans added to Arabian nobility.
-Hair and Earrings have been separated in the barbershop.
-Male Iranian Royal Crowns given to women as an option in barbershop.
-Iranian Christians will have their own clothing patterns and attire to differentiate from Zoroastrian and Islamic.


MISC CHANGES:

-Eye texture quality improved to remove some pixelation that occured through successive patches.
-All skin textures repainted and exported to remove pixelation that occured through successive patches.
-Complexions 38-45 added.
-Indian and Asian complexions temporarily overwritten until they can be properly repainted.
-Portrait Saturation slightly reduced
-Hindu Shahi modifier replaced with general modifier that makes Hindu Iranians wear Indo-Aryan clothing.

ETHNICITY CHANGES:

-Various ethnicity addtions across various cultures.

BUG FIXES:

-Beauty trait no longer makes lips puckered. The Beauty traits still affect facial shape but should not make every woman look like a duck face.
-All ethnicities should be in the proper numerial order in the Ruler Designer scrollbar.

##########################

Bug Fixes:

-Mod brought in line with mainline EPE and CFP.

Update: 14 Feb, 2022 @ 8:24pm

We are happy to announce a content update for EPE! Patch Notes Below! @everyone

##############################
Patch Notes: 1.1.7.0 - Genes, Barbershop, Customization, Oh my!
##############################

MAJOR UPDATE #1:

-Introducing a whole new set of genes for character customization! Our team has recently learned how to implement these quickly and we have a lot more ideas for the future.

List of new genes:

gene_bs_eye_outer_width
gene_bs_head_asymmetry
gene_bs_eye_fold_2
gene_bs_mouth_center_curve
gene_bs_eyebrow_straight
gene_bs_head_round_shape
gene_bs_nose_septum_height
gene_bs_head_lower_height
gene_bs_nose_flared_nostril
gene_bs_mouth_upper_lip_forward
gene_bs_mouth_lower_lip_forward
gene_bs_nose_swollen
gene_bs_ears_fantasy
gene_bs_mouth_glamour_lips

Most (not all) of these genes will naturally spawn on characters. Genes like asymmetry will not be as drastic on characters who spawn with the beauty traits, being only slightly present on comely characters, less on pretty/handsome characters, and non-existent on beautiful characters.

MAJOR UPDATE #2:

We have been asked for a long time to figure out a way to make cosmetics selectable in the barbershop. Well, thanks to AJ and the wonderful Elder Kings II team, we have done it! All makeup and cosmetics will be selectable in the barbershop!

UPDATE #1:

We have restored the values of the mouth_width gene and the eye_size_pos gene back to their 1.4.4 values. This means the bug eye appearance of characters should be gone and 1.4.4 DNA should work properly in the game.

UPDATE #2:

For some reason, the Royal Court items were not available in the Barbershop unless you used a mod like CFP. The vanilla game did not have them available. We have added them under “Special Headwear” in the barbershop for those who have the DLC.


Hotfixes:

-Fixed some custom genes that were broken by the Royal Court update.

-Fixed conflict with Fullscreen Barbershop. Characters should use proper prop swords with that mod enabled.

-Indian Crowns added by Royal Court should spawn more commonly on Northern Indians.

-The new MENA Turban added by Royal Court should spawn on High Medieval Islamic Iranians.

-Fixed Mena legwear spawning on military uniforms for all cultures.

-Fixed all remaining startup errors.

-Fixed odd values present for some ethnicities.

-Fixed minor headwear error with East Africans.

-Fixed outdated language localizations.

##############################
Community Flavor Pack Patch:
##############################

Bug Fixes:

-Brought mod in-line with current build of CFP
